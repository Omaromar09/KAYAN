Frequently Contemplated Inquiries

Find prompt answers regarding our ordering protocols, carbon-neutral logistics, bespoke alterations, and noble fiber longevity.

We provide Complimentary Carbon-Neutral Express Delivery / شحن سريع مجاني on all orders over 2,500 EGP (2,500 ج.م). Orders beneath this threshold incur a flat 75 EGP standard shipping fee. Transit times generally average 1–3 business days across Egypt and 2–4 business days internationally. Orders placed before 18:00 qualify for same-day atelier packing.

We offer complimentary doorstep pickup for returns within 30 days of acquisition receipt. Garments must remain unworn, unaltered, with original security tags and cedar packaging intact. Simply visit your Collector Portal or contact WhatsApp concierge to schedule a private courier collection.

During our Equinox seasonal event, acquiring 2 silhouettes applies an automated 20% reduction across both items. Acquiring 3 or more silhouettes unlocks a 25% reduction across the entire bag. No code is required; discounts calculate dynamically in your bag drawer.

Our garments are conceived with architectural presence, often featuring gentle dropped shoulders, intentional volume, and fluid drapes. Use our interactive Size Guide Calculator on any garment page to compute tailored fits based on bust and waist dimensions. Members of KAYAN Circle also enjoy complimentary hem and sleeve alterations at all 12 flagship ateliers.

Pure merino wool and cashmere should not be frequently laundered; wool naturally breathes and repels odors. Air garments after wear on the provided cedar hangers. For deep cleansing, employ specialist eco dry-cleaning only. Mulberry silk should be gently steamed or cold-washed with silk elixir.

The Circle comprises Member, Silver Horizon, Gold Solstice, and Obsidian Circle tiers. Points accrue at 1 point per 50 EGP acquired. Privileges include 48-hour pre-drop runway reserve, dedicated 24/7 private WhatsApp concierge, and private salon suite bookings.
